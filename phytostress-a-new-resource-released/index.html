PhytoStress - a new resource released!

New resource for plant research.

TopicsResourceAbiotic StressPlant NutritionGRN

The PhytoStress database is released now. Welcome to visit, test, and provide feedback. Contacted by Xianqing.

To translate the IPN concept into a practical research resource, we developed PhytoStress, an integrated platform for organizing, analyzing, and exploring plant nutrition data across nutrients, species, and data types.

Link: https://phytostress.plant-idea.cc
