OUR APPROACH

Our Lab focuses on understanding how plants adapt to environmental and nutritional challenges. We study plant Evo-Devo, nutrient signaling networks, and plant-environment interactions, and use these insights to guide the design of programmable plant systems. Our long-term goal is to uncover the principles of plant adaptive biology and translate them into innovative strategies for sustainable agriculture and environmental resilience.
